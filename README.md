# Online Retail Sales Analysis (Power BI)

## Project Overview

This project analyzes an online retail dataset using Power BI to uncover sales performance, customer behavior, and geographic revenue patterns.

The dashboard was designed for business stakeholders who need a quick overview of revenue trends, customer value, and country\-level performance.

## Business Questions

- How much revenue did the business generate?
- How many orders were placed?
- How many unique customers made purchases?
- What is the average order value?
- Which countries generate the most revenue?
- How concentrated is revenue among top customers?
- How frequently do customers return for additional purchases?

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Excel / CSV Data Source

## Dashboard Pages

### 1\. Executive Summary

Provides a high\-level overview of business performance.

Key metrics:

- Total Revenue: €10.67M
- Total Orders: 19,960
- Unique Customers: 4,338
- Average Order Value: €534
- Repeat Purchase Rate: 65.61%
- Average Revenue per Customer
- Average Customer Lifetime Value
- Average Orders per Customer

Visualizations:

- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Revenue by Country
- KPI Scorecards
- Date Filters

### 2\. Country Deep Dive

Allows users to explore sales performance by country.

Features:

- Interactive country selection
- Revenue comparison by country
- Country\-level customer metrics
- Orders and revenue breakdown

Business value:

- Identify top\-performing markets
- Compare customer behavior across countries
- Support international growth decisions

### 3\. Customer Analysis

Focuses on customer purchasing behavior.

Visualizations:

- Distribution of customers by order count
- Top 10 customers by revenue
- Revenue vs. Orders scatter plot

Insights:

- Most customers place a small number of orders
- A small group of customers contributes a large share of revenue
- High\-value customers can be identified for retention campaigns

## DAX Measures Created

Examples include:

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Unique Customers
- Average Order Value
- Revenue per Customer
- Average Customer Lifetime Value
- Repeat Purchase Rate
- Orders per Customer

## Key Insights

- Revenue exceeded €10 million during the observation period.
- The United Kingdom generated the largest share of revenue.
- Customer loyalty is strong, with a repeat purchase rate above 65%.
- Revenue is concentrated among a relatively small number of high\-value customers.
- Customer purchasing behavior follows a long\-tail distribution.
